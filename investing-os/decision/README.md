# Decision System

The decision system turns cognition and research into structured judgment.

It does not decide automatically.

It forces the user to clarify:

- thesis
- falsification
- position role
- risk budget
- allowed actions
- no-trade conditions

## Purpose

Prevent research, signals, or AI output from jumping directly into action.

## Files

- `portfolio-thesis.md`: current portfolio-level logic.
- `position-roles.md`: role of each holding or watched symbol.
- `risk-budget.md`: risk and attention budget boundaries.
- `falsification-map.md`: what would prove each thesis wrong.
- `current-watchlist.md`: symbols under review and their allowed action state.
- `decision-log.md`: important judgment changes and why they happened.

## Nutrient Sources

This system absorbs from:

- company dossiers
- industry dossiers
- valuation work
- pre-market plans
- post-market reviews
- stock_team packets
- cognition updates

## Output

Decision outputs feed:

- `execution/`: what can or cannot be done
- `system/data/snapshots/`: future approved runtime boundaries
- `evolution/`: review of decision quality
