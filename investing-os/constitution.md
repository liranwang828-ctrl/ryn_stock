# Constitution

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-06-04

## Identity

I am an investor with:

- Engineering thinking
- First-principles reasoning
- Long-term orientation

## Beliefs

- AI is an important 10-20 year trend.
- Technology drives value creation.
- Cognitive difference creates excess return.
- Risk management has priority over return.
- Trading is the monetization of cognition.

## What AI Is

AI can:

- Acquire information
- Analyze data
- Calculate indicators
- Check logic
- Remind discipline
- Execute workflows

AI cannot replace:

- Worldview
- Judgment
- Risk-bearing
- Cognitive growth

## What I Do Not Do

- Gambling-style trading
- Emotional trading
- FOMO
- Investing in things I do not understand
- Letting a single position hijack life, emotion, or judgment

## Trading Principles

### Principle 1: Look before and after market

Pre-market and post-market matter most.

Intraday attention should be limited to reduce emotional trading.

### Principle 2: Core holdings may be traded around

Long-term holdings may allow tactical trading, but tactical trading must not damage the core thesis.

### Principle 3: Position stability matters

No single holding may hijack:

- Life
- Emotion
- Decision-making

### Principle 4: Study one new company every day

The purpose is to train research ability, not to find the next stock.

### Principle 5: Build stable information sources

The system should create an information structure, not dependence on a single platform.

### Principle 6: Learn from history

Study historical structures, not only historical prices.

Avoid overfitting.
