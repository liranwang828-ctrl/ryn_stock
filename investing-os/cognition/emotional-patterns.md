# Emotional Patterns

## Purpose

Track emotional states that alter decision quality.

## Known Patterns

### Stable Long-Term Holding State

Observed:

- core holdings with researched thesis create emotional stability during market weakness

Useful because:

- thesis reduces noise sensitivity
- position role protects against intraday overreaction

### Short-Term Attention Capture

Observed:

- a low-conviction short-term trade can capture more attention than core holdings

Risks:

- sleep damage
- emotional spillover
- unplanned averaging down
- unrelated position decisions

Controls:

- cognitive attention budget
- 30-minute pause rule
- fact / thesis / risk restart

### Opportunity To Bailout Drift

Observed:

- a trade can start as an opportunity and become a cost-reduction or bailout project after price moves against it

Emotional drivers:

- FOMO at entry
- loss aversion after drawdown
- discomfort with being wrong quickly

Controls:

- write invalidation before entry
- write add rule before entry
- if add rule is missing, drawdown cannot justify adding

### Spillover Risk

Observed:

- stress from one short-term trade may affect decisions in unrelated holdings

Controls:

- before selling unrelated positions, classify decision as thesis change, risk management, or emotional spillover
- if classification is unclear and action is non-urgent, defer to review

### Relief To Re-Risking

Observed:

- after partial profitable exits, relief can create permission to continue or rebuild a low-quality trade

Risk:

- the user experiences the trade as "not so bad now"
- the system forgets that the original entry lacked thesis

Controls:

- partial recovery does not reset trade quality
- re-entry requires a fresh plan
- if the original trade had no thesis, recovery should bias toward reducing risk, not rebuilding it

### Hope Override

Observed:

- the user can notice weakness and still continue because of hope for reversal or outperformance

Risk:

- perception is working, but execution discipline is not
- the issue becomes obedience to evidence, not information quality

Controls:

- when weakness is noticed, pause before any add
- force written answer: what evidence would make this trade stop now?
- no add while the main reason is "maybe it reverses"

### Profit Streak Overconfidence

Observed:

- recent profits can create a feeling that short-term skill has improved
- a large winner can dominate P/L and hide luck / beta contribution
- several small short-term wins can make a tactic feel higher-confidence than evidence supports

Risk:

- discipline becomes looser after success
- leverage feels justified because the tactic appears high win-rate
- market-regime change is underweighted
- the user may keep applying a supportive-regime tactic after the market turns hostile

Controls:

- after a profit streak, decompose profit into skill, beta, luck, and one-large-winner
- do not increase leverage or short-term size immediately after a strong streak
- require market-regime confirmation before reusing a short-term tactic
- ask: would this tactic still work if QQQ / sector / VIX turned against me?

### Repair Desire After Short-Term Failure

Observed:

- after a short-term stop-loss, the user may continue watching and look for the next rebound
- the next setup can feel like opportunity but actually serve the prior loss

Risk:

- the trader is still in the old trade psychologically
- same tactic is reused after evidence has deteriorated
- re-entry becomes emotional repair

Controls:

- after any failed short-term trade and stop-loss, enter no-new-short-term-trade mode
- allow only risk reduction or prewritten swing-plan execution
- require a journal note before any later active trade

## Review Questions

- What emotion was strongest today?
- Did emotion change position handling?
- Did emotion change research quality?
- Did emotion affect sleep or next-day cognition?
- Did one position affect decisions in unrelated positions?
- Did a trade change from opportunity to bailout?
- Did partial recovery lead to re-risking?
- Did I notice weakness but continue because of hope?
- Did recent profits make me loosen discipline?
- Did I separate skill, beta, luck, and one-large-winner before sizing up?
- Was the next trade after a short-term loss really opportunity, or repair desire?
