# Cognition Core

This is the center of `investing-os`.

It describes how the user thinks, where judgment comes from, how mistakes happen, and what should evolve.

`wiki/` stores knowledge.

`cognition/` stores the user's self-model and thinking patterns.

## Purpose

Convert trading and research experience into better self-understanding.

Core questions:

- Who am I as an investor?
- Where do I have edge?
- Where do I repeatedly fail?
- What emotional states distort my judgment?
- What biases turn information into bad decisions?
- What kind of research actually improves my cognition?

## Files

- `self-model.md`: identity, strengths, weaknesses, and current investor profile.
- `bias-map.md`: recurring cognitive biases and how they appear.
- `emotional-patterns.md`: emotional states that affect trading and research.
- `mistake-patterns.md`: repeated decision errors and their triggers.
- `strengths-and-weaknesses.md`: durable advantages and fragile zones.

## Nutrient Sources

This system absorbs from:

- daily journals
- post-market reviews
- failed trades
- high-quality judgments
- research breakthroughs
- repeated emotional patterns
- stock_team packet reviews

## Output

Cognition should feed:

- `decision/`: better thesis, risk, and falsification judgment
- `execution/`: better constraints and no-trade rules
- `evolution/`: better principles and checklists
