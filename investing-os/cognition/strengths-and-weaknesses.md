# Strengths And Weaknesses

## Strengths

- can hold researched long-term positions through normal volatility
- can reason from first principles
- can distinguish technology trend from short-term price noise when thesis exists
- values risk management over immediate return
- willing to review mistakes directly
- can identify when a trade has become emotionally contaminated
- willing to convert painful losses into system rules rather than self-attack

## Weaknesses

- short-term opportunities can trigger FOMO
- low-conviction trades can receive excessive attention
- thesis can be created after action if signal feels urgent
- planless add behavior can appear during drawdown
- recent profits can create overconfidence before edge is proven
- short-term stop-loss can leave a repair impulse that contaminates the next trade
- broad thesis conviction can become fused with unsuitable leveraged instruments
- market-regime change can be underweighted when a tactic recently worked

## Development Focus

- make thesis explicit before action
- define risk before entry
- protect attention like capital
- convert review lessons into system updates
- decompose profits into skill, beta, luck, and one-large-winner before sizing up
- use permission states after loss, not only checklists before entry
- separate thesis, underlying, and instrument before holding leveraged products
