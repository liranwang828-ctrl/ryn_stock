# Company Research: 14 Dimensions

## Purpose

Study one company at a time to train judgment.

The goal is not to let AI produce a buy or sell conclusion.

The goal is to improve the user's own ability to understand businesses, industries, valuation, narrative, and falsification.

## Research Order

1. One-sentence company positioning
2. Revenue structure
3. Profit and cash-flow quality
4. Industry position
5. Moat
6. Pricing power
7. Competitive landscape
8. Management and capital allocation
9. Current market narrative
10. Stock behavior and volatility profile
11. Technical structure
12. Valuation and expectations
13. Key catalysts
14. Key falsification points

## AI Assistance Boundary

AI may:

- provide research questions
- suggest data sources and reading order
- provide counterarguments
- suggest historical analogies
- organize the user's own conclusions

AI must not:

- directly answer whether to buy
- replace the user's research process with a generic report
- produce a strong conclusion before the user forms judgment
