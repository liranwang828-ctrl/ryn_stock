### Note 2: Rebound Day Two Looks For Breadth

Observation:

```text
The first rebound day after a selloff often rewards the names hit hardest.
The second day should be judged by whether recovery broadens.
```

Principle:

```text
Rebound day one asks who is strongest.
Rebound day two asks whether strength spreads.
```

Healthy pattern:

- strongest names hold gains
- quality laggards begin recovering
- QQQ remains stronger than SPY
- market breadth improves

Weak pattern:

- only a small group of high-beta names keeps rising
- weaker names remain weak
- indexes rise without breadth
- leaders show intraday exhaustion

### Note 3: One-Day Strength Is Not Structural Proof

Principle:

```text
Do not treat one day of relative strength as structural market selection.
First check prior-day drawdown, base effect, independent catalysts, and multi-day confirmation.
```

Confirmation requires:

- repeated relative strength across more than one session
- evidence beyond simple oversold repair
- independent fundamental or catalyst support
- market breadth or sector confirmation
- no contradiction from rate / liquidity pressure
