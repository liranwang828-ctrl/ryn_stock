## Basic Data

Type:

- pre-market bullish plan
- strong open
- first entry with FOMO
- later pullback entries with better quality

## Facts

The user planned before market open to buy CRDU.

The user was broadly bullish and the overnight chart was strong.

After a strong open, the first entry occurred around 75.

That first buy had research support, but it also reflected fear of missing out.

Later entries around 68, 64.5, and 65.5 were higher quality because they occurred after pullbacks.

## Decision Context

The idea itself was not random.

There was pre-market preparation and positive context.

The problem was execution quality:

- the first entry was rushed
- price was extended away from VWAP
- the trade risked confusing thesis confidence with entry quality

## Subjective Psychology

Observed psychological pattern:

- fear of missing a planned opportunity
- urgency caused by strong open
- pressure to participate immediately
- later recognition that better entries required patience

## Objective Audit

What went right:

- there was prior research and a plan
- the user did not abandon the idea after the first imperfect entry
- later pullback entries improved cost basis and execution quality

What went wrong:

- first entry was too urgent
- entry occurred after price had moved far from VWAP
- position size on first entry needed stricter limits

## Falsification Or Missed Signals

When a stock opens strong but extended from VWAP, the first entry should be limited.

Larger position should wait for:

- pullback confirmation
- lower-volume support
- VWAP proximity or reclaim
- second breakout with renewed volume

## Principle Updates

This case supports:

- `PRIN-002: Hand-Feel Trade Limit`
- `PRIN-005: VWAP And Volume Confirmation Rules`

Rule reinforced:

The first FOMO entry does not need to be forbidden completely, but it must be position-limited.

## System Updates

Add to trade plan:

- Is the first entry extended from VWAP?
- Is this entry thesis-based or urgency-based?
- What portion of intended size is allowed before pullback confirmation?

## One Sentence Lesson

A good thesis does not justify a rushed first entry; keep real size for confirmed pullbacks or structure.
