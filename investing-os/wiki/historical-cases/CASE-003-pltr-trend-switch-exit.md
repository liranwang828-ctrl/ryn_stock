## Basic Data

Type:

- mainline switch
- attention transfer
- weak-position liquidation
- exit quality review

## Facts

The user exited PLTR.

The stated reason was that the day's mainline was not AI software and was unlikely to return quickly.

PLTR had weakened and was far below VWAP.

The user wanted to redirect capital and attention toward optical module opportunities.

## Decision Context

The motivation was understandable:

- mainline had shifted
- attention was limited
- capital efficiency mattered
- PLTR was weak relative to the active theme

The key issue was execution quality:

The exit occurred near the intraday low, making the trade-off between correct motivation and poor execution price important to review.

## Subjective Psychology

Observed psychological pattern:

- desire to simplify attention
- desire to free capital for the active theme
- impatience with a weak holding
- possible emotional pressure from watching a non-mainline stock lag

## Objective Audit

What went right:

- the user recognized mainline shift
- attention management was a valid consideration
- weak relative structure was noticed

What went wrong:

- exit price quality was poor
- the process may not have distinguished preplanned exit triggers from emotional reaction
- a weak rebound or predefined stop may have provided cleaner execution if the exit was not urgent

## Falsification Or Missed Signals

Direct liquidation is more justified when:

- the stock had already risen significantly in prior sessions
- it breaks VWAP with volume
- it is clearly weak relative to QQQ or the sector
- it cannot quickly reclaim VWAP
- the mainline has shifted and capital/attention must be reallocated

If these were not predefined, the action should be reviewed as possible emotional reaction.

## Principle Updates

This case supports:

- `PRIN-005: VWAP And Volume Confirmation Rules`

Potential future principle:

- exit discipline for mainline switch and attention management

## System Updates

Add to exit review:

- Was the exit preplanned or reactive?
- Was the motivation correct but the execution poor?
- Was attention management a real constraint or a rationalization?
- Could a weak rebound have improved execution without increasing risk?

## One Sentence Lesson

A correct reason to exit can still have poor execution quality; review motivation and execution separately.
