## Basic Data

Type:

- heavy position
- strong trend day
- profit-taking regret loop
- attention management

## Facts

COHR moved strongly within the optical module theme.

The user had experienced significant psychological pressure the prior day.

After the stock rose sharply, the user began taking profits in batches.

After the first profit-taking action, the stock continued rising.

This triggered regret about selling early and increased the urge to keep selling into higher prices.

## Decision Context

The original direction was broadly correct.

Taking partial profit was reasonable because the position was large and psychological pressure was high.

The key issue was not whether any profit-taking was allowed.

The key issue was whether follow-up selling was driven by structure or by regret.

## Subjective Psychology

Observed psychological pattern:

- fear of giving back large gains
- desire to lock in oversized profit
- regret after the first sale continued to rise
- attention shifted from trend quality to whether the exact high was captured
- follow-up selling risked becoming emotional peak-chasing in reverse

## Objective Audit

What went right:

- thesis direction was correct
- partial de-risking reduced psychological pressure
- profit protection had a rational basis

What went wrong:

- early profit-taking ratio may have been too high
- follow-up selling was affected by regret
- remaining position did not fully participate in the strong trend
- the process did not sufficiently separate first de-risking from subsequent exit logic

## Falsification Or Missed Signals

Further selling should have required structural evidence, such as:

- VWAP break
- moving take-profit break
- second target reached
- sector weakness
- volume-price structure distortion

Without these triggers, regret alone was not a valid sell signal.

## Principle Updates

This case supports:

- `PRIN-003: Profit-Taking Cooling Period`

Rule reinforced:

After first profit-taking, enter a cooling period before discretionary follow-up selling.

## System Updates

Add to post-trade review:

- Was follow-up selling structural or emotional?
- Did first profit-taking release pressure or create regret?
- Did the user preserve a trend-following remainder?

## One Sentence Lesson

Taking partial profit can be rational, but regret after taking profit is not a new sell signal.
