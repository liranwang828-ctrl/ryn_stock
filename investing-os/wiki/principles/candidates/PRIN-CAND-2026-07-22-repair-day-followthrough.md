# Principle Candidate: Repair Day Follow-Through Test

## Candidate Status

- Status: candidate
- Created: 2026-07-22
- Source: 2026-07-21 AI / semiconductor repair session review

## Core Assertion

After a sharp selloff, a strong repair day should be treated as a follow-through test of prior thesis damage, not as automatic thesis confirmation.

## Why This Candidate Exists

Large rebounds often tempt the system into one of two symmetrical mistakes:

- calling a repair day full thesis confirmation;
- calling the next overnight cooldown immediate repair failure.

Both are too fast.

The correct sequence is:

1. identify whether the repair was broad or role-concentrated;
2. identify which layer repaired: Reality / Demand / Commercialization / Verification;
3. observe the next overnight and next session for follow-through or rejection;
4. only then upgrade thesis strength.

## Working Interpretation Rules

### Rule 1

If repair is concentrated in Hardware / Demand / Power / Verification names while Software lags, treat it as evidence that hardware-related Reality may still be intact.

### Rule 2

If the next overnight session cools ahead of major earnings or macro events, do not automatically classify that as repair failure.

### Rule 3

If Verification names repair sharply, interpret that first as:

- lower market doubt
- repricing
- re-rating

not immediate Reality confirmation.

### Rule 4

The most informative stocks on a repair day are:

- names that should repair and do repair;
- names that should repair but do not;
- names that repair only because of one isolated catalyst.

## What This Candidate Forbids

- Treating one repair day as full thesis validation
- Treating one softer overnight as proof the repair was fake
- Promoting Verification Layer names to Reality Layer because of one strong session
- Ignoring sector role separation during a rebound

## Current Example

2026-07-21:

- SOXX +5.45%
- AMAT +7.39%
- MU +12.17%
- NBIS +18.78%
- MSFT -1.13%
- NOW -2.52%
- GOOGL -1.38%

Interpretation:

- the repair was concentrated in hardware / AI infrastructure / verification;
- software did not take leadership;
- this supported the view that prior weakness was largely price compression rather than immediate hardware Reality collapse.
