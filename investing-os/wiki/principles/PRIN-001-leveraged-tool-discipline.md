## Core Assertion

Leveraged products are high-elasticity expression tools.

They are not ordinary long-term holdings.

## Why It Exists

Leveraged products can magnify correct short-term cognition, but they also magnify thesis drift, emotional holding, and delayed exits.

This principle exists to prevent a tactical leveraged position from silently becoming a long-term position.

## Trigger Conditions

Apply this principle whenever using a leveraged or high-elasticity instrument, including but not limited to CRDU, INTW, AAOX, or similar products.

Use cases may include:

- intraday trend expression
- short swing enhancement
- temporary expression of a high-conviction short-term opportunity

## Execution Constraints

Every leveraged-tool trade must record:

- underlying asset
- leverage ratio or product structure
- trade thesis
- stop condition
- target condition
- maximum holding time
- whether the trade is intraday or short swing

Stops must be tighter than ordinary holdings.

If the underlying asset breaks a key structure, the leveraged tool should exit first.

## What This Principle Forbids

- Treating a leveraged tool as a normal long-term position
- Allowing a leveraged position to exceed speculative position limits
- Holding a leveraged product after the underlying thesis has weakened
- Increasing a leveraged position because of regret or FOMO
- Using leverage when the underlying structure is unclear

## Falsification Or Revision Conditions

Revise this principle only if repeated reviews show a clearly defined leveraged-tool process improves cognition-adjusted returns without increasing emotional risk, life disruption, or drawdown risk.

Do not revise it based on one profitable exception.

## Drift Audit

- Did the trade start as tactical but become long-term?
- Was the stop moved because of hope?
- Did leverage change the user's emotional state?
- Was the underlying structure checked, or only the leveraged product price?
- Did the position affect sleep or daily decision-making?
