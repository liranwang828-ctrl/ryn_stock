## Core Assertion

Hand-feel trades are small tactical trades for intraday market-feel training.

They are not substitutes for thesis-driven investing.

## Why It Exists

The user wants to train market sense without letting tactical curiosity become emotional overtrading.

This principle keeps hand-feel trades small, time-bounded, and reviewable.

## Trigger Conditions

Apply this principle to any small intraday tactical trade entered mainly for:

- tape reading
- chart and volume practice
- short-term tactical opportunity
- market-feel training

## Execution Constraints

Every hand-feel trade must record:

- whether the chart is clear
- whether volume confirms
- whether VWAP position is reasonable
- whether it occurs in the preferred opening window
- whether it is mainline, sympathy follow-through, or pure emotion-following

Preferred timing:

- mainly in the first 1-2 hours after market open
- default no new hand-feel trades in the latter half of the session
- late-session exceptions require a new catalyst or new structure

## What This Principle Forbids

- Trading just because "it also moved"
- Trading without chart, volume, and VWAP confirmation
- Adding tactical trades after the daily profit objective has already been reached
- Trading when a core volatile position already occupies attention
- Expanding hand-feel trades into meaningful portfolio risk

## Falsification Or Revision Conditions

Revise timing and limits only after reviewing a statistically meaningful sample of hand-feel trades.

Do not revise based on one missed late-session opportunity.

## Drift Audit

- Was this really training, or was it disguised FOMO?
- Did the trade occur after emotional profit or loss?
- Was there chart, volume, and VWAP confirmation?
- Did the trade distract from core holdings?
- Did the trade size exceed its training purpose?
