## One-Sentence Positioning

AI GPU compute may be a high-growth but time-bounded infrastructure window, with major opportunities and risks concentrated in compute-as-a-service, neo-clouds, hyperscalers, power availability, GPU depreciation, and customer concentration.

## Core Thesis From Source

The source argued:

- AI GPU compute has a 2-3 year golden window rather than a stable 5-year opportunity.
- Neo-clouds may benefit during the infrastructure shortage window, but the model is capital intensive and fragile.
- Hyperscalers may strengthen over time through self-built infrastructure and ASICs.
- NBIS, IREN, and CRWV were the core watch names, but none should automatically become core holdings.
- Leveraged NBIS exposure through NBIL was explicitly rejected in the source.

Examples:

Source view:

## Value Capture Questions

- Who controls scarce GPUs?
- Who controls power?
- Who has long-term contracted customers?
- Who can finance capex without fragile debt?
- Who survives GPU depreciation cycles?
- Who benefits if inference shifts from GPU to ASIC?

## Key Risks

- AI model efficiency breakthroughs reducing compute demand
- hyperscaler self-developed ASICs
- rapid GPU obsolescence
- power bottlenecks
- customer concentration
- high interest expense and leverage
- regulatory and export controls

## Falsification

This industry thesis weakens if:

- AI capex guidance materially declines
- inference growth shifts decisively to ASICs
- GPU rental economics compress faster than expected
- neo-cloud financing becomes unavailable
- major customers internalize workloads
- second-hand GPU prices collapse faster than depreciation assumptions

## Monitoring Checklist

- OpenAI / Anthropic / xAI capex guidance
- DeepSeek-like efficiency events
- Trainium / Maia / TPU adoption
- H100 / H200 secondary-market pricing
- NVIDIA data-center growth and guidance
- CRWV customer concentration and interest coverage
- NBIS capex financing
- IREN data-center milestones and customer mix

## Open Questions

- Which neo-clouds have real differentiation beyond NVIDIA access?
- Does power scarcity make energy-backed compute providers structurally advantaged?
- When does GPU depreciation overwhelm rental economics?
