## Purpose

Build a stable map for deciding when to look at the market, when to look at sectors, and when to look at individual stocks.

## Market Layer

Monitor:

- QQQ
- SPY
- VIX
- rates
- dollar
- BTC
- key macro data

Core questions:

- Is today an offensive day or defensive day?
- Who is stronger when QQQ rises?
- Who is resilient when QQQ pulls back?
- Is risk appetite rising or falling?

## AI Rotation Layer

AI is not one bucket.

Capital can rotate inside AI:

- Software / security / application layer: NOW, PLTR, APP, SNOW, ZS
- Hardware / semiconductor / storage: NVDA, AVGO, MRVL, MU, SNDK
- Optical modules: COHR, LITE, AAOI
- Power / energy: VST, CEG, OKLO
- High-beta themes: CRWV, NBIS, RKLB, ASTS

## Core Questions

- Is capital leaving AI, or rotating inside AI?
- Is the current mainline software, hardware, optical modules, storage, power, or high-beta sentiment?
- Is the catalyst industry-level, sector-level, or company-specific?
