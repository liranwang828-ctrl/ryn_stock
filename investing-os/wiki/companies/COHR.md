## One-Sentence Positioning

COHR is a swing candidate for AI datacenter optical bandwidth demand: it offers broad photonics platform exposure, but it is valid only when the market rewards that breadth rather than preferring cleaner optical peers.

## Business Model

Coherent develops and manufactures photonics, engineered materials, optical components, transceivers, modules, subsystems, and laser systems.

Local company profile describes three major areas:

- Networking: transceivers, modules, optical components, subsystems, and semiconductor devices for datacenter and communications.
- Materials: engineered materials, laser optics, thermoelectric components, advanced ceramics, VCSELs, edge-emitting lasers, pump lasers, and related products.
- Lasers: excimer, solid-state, CO2, and other laser systems for industrial, semiconductor, display, and scientific use cases.

## Industry Context

AI datacenter scaling is creating demand for higher-bandwidth, lower-power optical interconnect.

The relevant bottlenecks are:

- 800G / 1.6T transceiver supply
- 200G/lane EML and InP capability
- co-packaged optics
- optical switching
- power efficiency
- reliability and hyperscale qualification

Simplified chain:

```text
hyperscaler AI capex
|
GPU clusters and networking systems
|
switches / optical interconnect / transceiver demand
|
optical components, lasers, InP/EML, SiPho, CPO, OCS
|
COHR / LITE / AAOI / other suppliers
```

## Falsification

### Business Falsification

The thesis weakens if:

- datacenter / communications demand decelerates materially
- management stops emphasizing AI datacenter capacity expansion
- COHR loses major hyperscale or networking opportunities to peers
- new optical architectures reduce COHR value capture

### Financial Falsification

The thesis weakens if:

- revenue growth remains strong but gross margin fails to improve
- capacity expansion creates cost pressure without revenue conversion
- cash-flow quality deteriorates
- debt or capex burden becomes the dominant story

### Market-Structure Falsification

The swing thesis weakens if:

- COHR lags LITE, AAOI, and SOXX during optical-led rallies
- COHR cannot reclaim key moving averages after sector strength
- COHR repeatedly fails near prior high zones while peers break out
- volume does not confirm upward moves

## Risk Controls

- No re-entry from regret about prior sale.
