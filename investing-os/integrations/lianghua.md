# Lianghua Integration

## Role

`lianghua` is the quantitative research and tooling lab.

`investing-os` is the cognition, discipline, review, and knowledge system.

## Boundary

`lianghua` may produce:

- Signals
- Indicators
- Backtests
- Data pipelines
- Strategy experiments
- Quantitative observations

`investing-os` may absorb:

- Validated lessons
- Risk rules
- Research workflows
- Failed assumptions
- Useful indicators
- Signal interpretation notes
- Methodology updates

## What Stays In Lianghua

- Raw data
- Backtest engines
- Strategy scripts
- Temporary notebooks
- Large generated outputs
- Experimental code

## What Enters Investing OS

Only durable cognition enters:

- Why a signal matters
- When a strategy fails
- What assumptions were wrong
- What risk rule should be added
- What thesis was strengthened or weakened
- What workflow should change

## Signal Rule

A signal from `lianghua` is not a trade command.

It can trigger:

- Review
- Research
- Risk check
- Thesis update
- Watchlist update
- Execution of an already-written plan

It must not directly trigger:

- Unplanned trades
- Emotional action
- Position sizing without risk review
- Human judgment bypass

## Required Translation Pattern

When importing a result from `lianghua`, translate it into:

```text
Source:
Signal or result:
Underlying assumption:
Why it matters:
What it changes:
What it does not prove:
Risk:
Falsification:
Action:
Wiki update:
```
