# Company Research Rebuild: COHR / ORCL / INTC

## Purpose

Correct the workflow after the 2026-06-04 exit review.

The current COHR / ORCL / INTC files are fast role/thesis drafts.

They are not complete company research dossiers.

## Boundary

Confirmed by user:

- COHR: swing / trend candidate
- ORCL: swing / trend candidate
- INTC: short-term tactical to swing candidate

Not confirmed:

- active trade permission
- full thesis adoption
- valuation frame
- sizing
- trigger
- invalidation
- time stop

## Required Process

Each symbol must go through:

```text
company research packet
|
packet review
|
packet absorption decision
|
company dossier update
|
thesis candidate decision
|
trade plan draft, if still valid
```

## Research Questions

### COHR

- Is COHR the best optical infrastructure expression versus LITE / AAOI / SOXX?
- Is COHR currently leading or lagging the optical peer basket?
- Is the value capture from photonics breadth strong enough to offset complexity discount?
- What trend break or peer-lag condition invalidates the swing?

### ORCL

- Is ORCL mainly AI cloud infrastructure capacity, AI software/platform rotation, or both?
- Is the $553B RPO high-quality and convertible into revenue and cash flow?
- Does capex / financing risk undermine the swing thesis?
- What evidence would show backlog excitement is already priced in?

### INTC

- Is INTC a short-term momentum/reversal trade or a real swing thesis?
- Is strength stock-specific or only semiconductor beta?
- What evidence upgrades it from short-term tactical to swing hold?
- What time stop applies if the catalyst does not work quickly?

## Required Outputs Per Symbol

1. `system/data/packets/drafts/company-research-{SYMBOL}-2026-06-05.md`
2. packet review record
3. updated `wiki/companies/{SYMBOL}.md`
4. `decision/current-watchlist.md` update
5. optional trade plan only after thesis candidate survives

## Safety Rule

Do not write a trade plan directly from the fast dossier.

The fast dossier can only seed the company research packet.
