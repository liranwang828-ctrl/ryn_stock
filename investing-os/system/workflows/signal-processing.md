# Signal Processing Workflow

Signals are inputs to cognition, not commands.

## Signal Types

### Information Signal

Examples:

- Earnings
- Filings
- News
- Management changes
- Industry policy
- Product announcements

### Market Signal

Examples:

- Price movement
- Volume anomaly
- Volatility change
- Trend shift
- Relative strength

### Thesis Signal

Examples:

- Evidence strengthens thesis
- Evidence weakens thesis
- Assumption is challenged
- Falsification condition approaches

### Risk Signal

Examples:

- Position concentration
- Liquidity deterioration
- Correlation increase
- Emotional stress
- Sleep disruption

## Workflow

```text
Signal appears
|
Classify signal
|
Check relevance
|
Interpret meaning
|
Check thesis / valuation / risk impact
|
Check whether action is already planned
|
Choose allowed outcome
```

## Allowed Outcomes

- Ignore
- Add to research queue
- Update watchlist
- Update thesis
- Update risk note
- Prepare trade plan
- Execute existing written plan

## Rule

New signals can trigger research.

New signals cannot trigger unplanned trades.

Exception:

Only a signal already defined in a written plan may trigger execution.
