# Industry Dossier Lifecycle

## Purpose

Define how industry research becomes durable industry knowledge.

This workflow is for converting industry packets, manual research, and stock-team generated sector reports into `wiki/industries/`.

## Core Rule

An industry note is not a buy list.

The goal is to understand:

- structural change
- value creation
- value capture
- bottlenecks
- historical analogies
- risks
- companies worth deeper research

## Lifecycle States

### 1. Theme Candidate

Use when:

- theme is interesting but not yet understood
- signal is driven by market narrative
- no value-chain map exists

Allowed outputs:

- research question
- source list
- company candidates for later study

Forbidden outputs:

- sector trade plan
- broad sympathy conclusion

### 2. Industry Map Draft

Use when:

- supply chain is partially mapped
- key layers are known
- bottlenecks are suspected

Allowed outputs:

- `wiki/industries/{industry}.md` draft
- value-chain map
- open questions

### 3. Value-Capture Thesis

Use when:

- industry value creation is understood
- likely profit pools are identified
- key beneficiaries and losers are named by role
- falsification is written

Allowed outputs:

- company research priorities
- monitoring plan
- historical analogy note

Forbidden outputs:

- automatic sector basket
- buy all peers behavior

### 4. Active Industry Dossier

Use when:

- value-chain map is reviewed
- bottlenecks are explicit
- company roles are clear
- monitoring plan exists
- risks and counter-thesis are documented

Allowed outputs:

- company dossier candidates
- watchlist metadata candidates
- pre-market context
- post-market thesis review

### 5. Obsolete Or Invalidated

Use when:

- structural thesis fails
- technology path changes
- value capture moves elsewhere
- market overprices the theme
- historical analogy proves misleading

Allowed outputs:

- historical case
- methodology update
- archived industry note

## Required Sections

Minimum durable industry dossier:

- worldview link
- first-principles analysis
- supply chain
- bottlenecks
- value creation
- value capture
- key companies by role
- historical analogy
- risks and counter-thesis
- monitoring plan
- open questions
- review due date

## Packet Intake

Industry research packets should go through:

- `system/workflows/packet-absorption.md`
- `templates/packet-review.md`

Only durable learning should enter:

- `wiki/industries/`
- `wiki/methodologies/`
- `wiki/companies/` as research tasks

## Review Triggers

Review an industry dossier when:

- major earnings cluster changes the thesis
- capex, demand, or supply-chain data changes
- policy/geopolitical constraints change
- leading companies diverge from the industry thesis
- market narrative becomes too consensus
- historical analogy stops fitting

## Promotion Rules

### Theme Candidate To Industry Map Draft

Requires:

- research question
- source list
- preliminary supply-chain map

### Industry Map Draft To Value-Capture Thesis

Requires:

- bottleneck analysis
- value-capture hypothesis
- company role map
- counter-thesis

### Value-Capture Thesis To Active Industry Dossier

Requires:

- reviewed monitoring plan
- explicit falsification
- company research priorities
- known overfitting risks

## Safety Boundary

Industry dossier status never authorizes sector trades.

It only improves:

- worldview
- research direction
- company prioritization
- context for pre-market and post-market review
