# Wiki Update Workflow

## Goal

Convert temporary context into durable knowledge.

## Steps

1. Identify what was learned.
2. Decide the correct wiki section.
3. Separate fact from interpretation.
4. Add sources when relevant.
5. Link related notes.
6. Record open questions.

## Rule

The wiki stores knowledge, not conversation context.

## Packet Inputs

If the source is a packet from `stock_team`, ChatGPT, or manual research, use:

- `system/workflows/packet-absorption.md`
- `templates/packet-review.md`

Do not update the wiki directly from an unreviewed packet.
