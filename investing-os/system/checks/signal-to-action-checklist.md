# Signal To Action Checklist

A signal is input to cognition.

A signal is not a command.

## Signal Classification

- Information signal
- Market signal
- Thesis signal
- Risk signal
- System signal

## Relevance

- Is it related to a holding?
- Is it related to the watchlist?
- Is it related to my worldview?
- Is it related to an active research question?

## Meaning

- What happened?
- Why might it matter?
- What assumption does it affect?
- Does it change thesis?
- Does it change valuation?
- Does it change risk?

## Action Eligibility

- Was this signal already included in the written plan?
- Is the action predefined?
- Is position size predefined?
- Is the exit condition predefined?
- Is the risk acceptable if I sleep through the market?

## Allowed Outcomes

- Ignore
- Add to research queue
- Update watchlist
- Update thesis
- Update risk note
- Prepare a trade plan
- Execute an existing written plan

## Forbidden Outcomes

- Unplanned trade
- Emotion-driven action
- Increasing risk without review
- Treating AI or code output as final judgment
