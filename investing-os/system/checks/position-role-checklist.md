# Position Role Checklist

Every trade or holding must have a role before action.

## Roles

- Long-term thesis holding
- Swing holding
- Intraday holding
- Hand-feel trade
- Leveraged / options holding
- Watchlist only
- Research only

## Three-Layer Portfolio Framework

### Base Layer

Purpose:

- long-term core assets
- stabilizer
- business-quality exposure

Examples:

- MSFT
- GOOGL
- NVDA
- broad index or mega-cap core exposure

Rules:

- thesis based on business model, industry position, financial quality, and long-term AI / cloud / platform narrative
- tactical trading is allowed only to optimize cost
- tactical trading must not convert long-term holdings into intraday emotional positions

### Swing Attack Layer

Purpose:

- main growth engine
- medium-term opportunity capture

Examples:

- COHR
- ORCL
- PLTR
- other high-quality stocks in strong mainlines

Rules:

- concentrate on the best 2-3 swing opportunities
- prefer clear thesis, strong mainline, relative strength, and sustained catalyst
- if one position consumes too much attention, reduce risk or write a clearer plan

### Learning / Speculative Layer

Purpose:

- options
- leveraged tools
- event trades
- hand-feel trades
- short-term experiments

Rules:

- used for learning and high-elasticity opportunities
- must not destabilize the account
- every trade needs role, stop, target, and maximum holding time
- should respect `PRIN-001`, `PRIN-002`, and `PRIN-006`

## Forbidden

- Upgrading intraday trades into long-term holdings during market hours
- Treating leveraged tools as ordinary stocks
- Letting speculative trades consume base-layer or swing-layer risk budget
- Acting before role is defined
