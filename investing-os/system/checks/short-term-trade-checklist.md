# Short-Term Trade Checklist

Use this before any short-term or intraday trade.

## Required Before Entry

- What is today's market regime?
- Are QQQ / SPY supportive, neutral, or risk-off?
- Is VIX stable/falling, or rising?
- Is the relevant sector ETF confirming?
- What is the thesis?
- Is this thesis explicit, or only an implicit theme view?
- Why this symbol rather than another expression of the theme?
- What is the intended time horizon?
- What is the trigger?
- What is the maximum loss?
- What invalidates the trade?
- What is the position size?
- What is the add rule?
- What is the exit rule?
- What is the maximum attention budget?
- What is the re-entry rule after a partial exit?

## Profit Streak Check

If recent trading has been profitable:

- How much came from repeatable process?
- How much came from broad market beta?
- How much came from one large winner?
- How much may have been luck?
- Am I using recent profit as permission to increase size or leverage?
- Would this tactic still work if QQQ / sector / VIX turned against me?

Rule:

Do not increase short-term size or leverage after a profit streak until profit sources are decomposed.

## Signal Check

- Is this only a signal?
- Am I treating VWAP, volume, or price movement as a full thesis?
- Is there catalyst, structure, and risk definition?

## Add Rule

Adding is allowed only if:

- it was defined before entry
- thesis is strengthened by new evidence
- risk remains within budget
- the add is not merely cost reduction
- the add is not based on hoping visible weakness reverses

## Attention Check

If I have watched the trade continuously for too long:

1. Stop watching.
2. Write facts.
3. Restate thesis.
4. Restate risk.
5. Decide whether the trade still matches the plan.

## Sleep Check

- Can I sleep with this position?
- Will this trade affect tomorrow's judgment?
- Is this low-conviction trade receiving high-conviction attention?

## Re-Risking Check

Before re-entering or rebuilding exposure after a partial profitable exit:

- Did the original trade have a thesis?
- Did new evidence improve the thesis?
- Or did partial recovery only improve emotion?
- Did the symbol or underlying sustain the high area?
- Is the trade below VWAP?
- Is it after 15:30 ET?
- Are QQQ/SPY fading into the close?
- Is there a fresh trigger?
- Is there a fresh invalidation?
- Is there a fresh add rule?
- Am I seeing weakness but hoping for reversal?

If not, do not rebuild exposure.

## Stop-Loss Cooldown

After any failed short-term trade and stop-loss:

- enter no-new-short-term-trade mode for the rest of the session
- allow only risk reduction or prewritten swing-plan execution
- do not re-enter the same tactic to recover the loss
- do not increase size because the rebound looks stronger
- write a journal note before any later active trade

Rule:

The next short-term trade after a stop-loss is presumed contaminated by repair desire unless proven otherwise by a prewritten plan.

## Leveraged Product Check

Before using a leveraged or daily-reset product:

- Is the underlying thesis valid?
- Is the underlying still showing relative strength?
- Is this leveraged instrument suitable for the holding period?
- Can the product survive the expected path volatility?
- What is the max dollar loss?
- What is the max account percentage loss?
- Is this position small enough not to affect sleep, work, or judgment?

Rule:

A valid theme thesis does not automatically validate a leveraged instrument.

## Spillover Check

Before changing unrelated holdings during or after a stressful trade:

- Would I make this same decision if the stressful trade did not exist?
- Is this thesis change, risk management, emotional spillover, or mixed?
- Is the process clean even if the outcome may be protective?

## Forbidden

- No plan, no trade.
- No thesis, no add.
- No implicit-theme trade without execution constraints.
- No signal-only trade with meaningful size.
- No cost-reduction averaging without prewritten add rule.
- No single low-conviction ticker hijacking the whole account.
- No re-risking after partial recovery without a fresh plan.
- No adding while visible weakness is being overridden by hope.
- No new short-term trade after a short-term stop-loss unless it was a prewritten swing-plan action or risk reduction.
- No leverage increase after profit streak without profit-source decomposition.
