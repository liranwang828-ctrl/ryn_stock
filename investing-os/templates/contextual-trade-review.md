## 1. Trade Identity

## 2. Market Context

What were SPY, QQQ, VIX, and relevant sector/peer behavior doing?

Separate:

- market trend
- market volatility
- risk-on / risk-off state
- sector confirmation
- market deterioration during the trade

## 3. Symbol Context

What was the traded symbol doing?

Include:

- opening structure
- VWAP relationship
- volume behavior
- failed breakouts
- reclaim attempts
- late-session breakdown
- special product mechanics

## 4. Fill Path

Summarize:

- first entry
- add points
- partial exits
- re-entry / re-risking
- final exit

## 5. Interview

Ask the trader:

1. Before the first entry, what exactly did you believe would happen?
2. What was the invalidation condition at entry?
3. What did each add mean: thesis strengthening, cost reduction, or emotional repair?
4. After partial profitable exits, why was exposure rebuilt?
5. At what moment did the trade start occupying disproportionate attention?
6. Did market weakness change the trade, or did the trade ignore market weakness?
7. Did this trade influence unrelated holdings?
8. If you could pause once, which timestamp should have triggered the pause?
