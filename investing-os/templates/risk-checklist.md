# Risk Checklist

## Position Risk

- Single-name concentration:
- Portfolio correlation:
- Liquidity:
- Event risk:
- Notional as % of account:
- Max additional loss:
- Max account % loss:
- Is margin used:
- Can this position affect sleep, work, or judgment:

## Thesis Risk

- What must remain true?
- What has changed?
- What would prove me wrong?

## Instrument Risk

- What is the underlying thesis?
- What is the underlying symbol / asset?
- What instrument expresses it?
- Is the instrument leveraged, daily-reset, option-like, or path-dependent?
- Is the holding period compatible with the instrument?
- Can the instrument survive the expected volatility path?

Rule:

Separate:

```text
theme thesis
|
underlying relative strength
|
instrument suitability
```

A thesis can remain valid while the instrument is invalid.

## Behavioral Risk

- Am I emotional?
- Am I trying to recover losses?
- Am I overconfident?
- Is this position affecting life or judgment?
- Did recent profit make me loosen discipline?
- Have I decomposed recent profit into skill / beta / luck / one large winner?
- Am I holding because thesis is strong, or because realizing loss is painful?
